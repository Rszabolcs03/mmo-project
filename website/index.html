The game

A world shaped by roads, ruins, and restless magic.

Wayworn is an old-school fantasy MMO built around crossroads, frontier towns, and the threats waking beneath them. Quest through race starting zones, level to the cap, pick a talent specialization, learn professions, and descend into the first dungeon with other players.

Six classes, each with its own way to fight.

The world

From frontier towns to what waits below.

The overworld is a growing continent of connected regions: quest hubs, wilderness, world bosses, and roads that actually lead somewhere. Along the way you will find vendors, banks, an auction house, and respawn shrines for when the road wins.

  • Starting zonesA hand-built home region for every race, with quests and towns.
  • Open worldSeamless regions with roaming enemies and world bosses.
  • Day & nightA living cycle with weather fronts that roll across the map.
  • EconomyVendors, personal bank storage, and a player auction house.

The studio

Bridgeward Studio started where many good quests start: at a local pub.

The name comes from a place close to home in Hungary — a pub called Hídfő, where many of the ideas behind the project took shape with a friend. Bridgeward keeps that feeling: crossing into something new, building from small beginnings, and following the road because the next zone might be worth it.
